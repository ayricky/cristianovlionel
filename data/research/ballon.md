# Ballon d'Or recount

Vote totals come from each year's Wikipedia results page, saved in `data/raw/ballon`. Player numbers come from the match logs, cut to each award's window in `data/pipeline/extras.py`.

Windows: January to November for 2009-2015 and 2018, January to October for 2019 and 2021, 1 August 2022 to 31 July 2023 for 2023. For 2012 the three finalists were named on 29 November, so the window stops there. Messi had 82 goals by that date and finished the calendar year on 91.

| Year | Winner | Ronaldo | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2009 | Messi 473, Ronaldo 233 | 2nd | Messi. Barcelona's treble |
| 2010 | Messi 22.65% | 6th | Messi over Ronaldo: 56 goals to 41. Iniesta and Sneijder were the real debate |
| 2011 | Messi 47.88%, Ronaldo 21.60% | 2nd | Close on scoring rate. Messi won the Champions League |
| 2012 | Messi 41.60%, Ronaldo 23.68% | 2nd | Strong case. League, Supercopa and a goal in all six Clásicos |
| 2015 | Messi 41.33%, Ronaldo 27.76% | 2nd | Messi. Treble, and Ronaldo won nothing in that window |
| 2018 | Modrić 753, Ronaldo 476 | 2nd | Strong case. Champions League winner and its top scorer |
| 2019 | Messi 686, van Dijk 679, Ronaldo 476 | 3rd | Strong case. Three trophies to one |
| 2021 | Messi 613, Lewandowski 580, Ronaldo 178 | 6th | Close. Two trophies each, Euro Golden Boot, 15 winners to 9 |
| 2023 | Messi 462, Haaland 357 | not placed | Messi. World Cup |

This is an argument about who deserved the vote, not a claim the votes were wrong on any stated rule. On raw goals Messi leads in 2012 and 2019, and the page says so.
