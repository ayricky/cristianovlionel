# Ronaldo vs Messi: honours and records fact check

Checked 5 November 2026. Numbers run through Ronaldo's last goals on 29 Oct 2026 and Messi's on 1 Nov 2026.

Status tags used below:

- **OK**: a primary body (UEFA, FIFA, league, club, Guinness) or a strong outlet (ESPN, Opta/The Analyst) states it, and it agrees with Wikipedia.
- **WIKI**: Wikipedia with citations is the best source I reached.
- **FAN**: I computed it from the fan site's own match lists (`ronaldo-match-history.json`, `messi-match-history.json`). Those lists tie out to 985 and 937 and to every club and competition total I compared, but they are still a fan source.
- **UNCONFIRMED**: I could not confirm it. Don't publish it as fact.

Wikipedia links point to live pages. I read the raw wikitext of each one on 5 Nov 2026. BBC, Reuters, AP and The Guardian refused my tools, so none of them is cited directly.

## Headlines

- Spain won the 2026 World Cup, 1-0 over Argentina after extra time. Argentina were runners-up. Portugal went out in the round of 16, also to Spain.
- Al Nassr won the 2025-26 Saudi Pro League. It is Ronaldo's first league title since 2020. He finished third in the scoring race.
- Ronaldo retired from Portugal today, 5 Nov 2026, on 146 goals in 234 caps. Messi retired from Argentina on 126 goals in 208 caps after a farewell friendly on 6 Oct. Both international totals are now final.
- Headline career goals: Ronaldo 985, Messi 937. The 985 includes one goal Opta calls an own goal and six goals Transfermarkt and Sofascore exclude. See section 5.
- Mbappe, not Messi, now holds the World Cup goals record, with 22.
- Harry Kane won the 2026 Ballon d'Or. Messi came 5th. Ronaldo was not on the shortlist.

### Why the counters disagree

1. **Al Faisaly, 23 Oct 2026, -1 goal.** The Saudi Pro League and the referee's report credit Ronaldo. Opta, Sofascore and FotMob call it an own goal by Andrei Girotto. [SI, 2 Nov](https://www.si.com/soccer/cristiano-ronaldo-race-1000-goals-unwelcome-twist), [beIN](https://www.beinsports.com/en-us/soccer/articles/cristiano-ronaldo-s-ghost-goals-spark-huge-al-nassr-controversy-2026-10-27). The base file counts it, so its 985 and 138 for Al Nassr include it. beIN said ESPN sided with Opta on 27 Oct, yet ESPN printed 985 two days later. I cannot explain that.
2. **Arab Club Champions Cup 2023, -6 goals.** FIFA does not list it as a major competition, so Transfermarkt and Sofascore drop it. [beIN says](https://www.beinsports.com/en-us/soccer/articles/cristiano-ronaldo-s-goal-tally-sparks-controversy-on-his-road-to-1000-2026-04-08) IFFHS counts it and FIFA's own 950th-goal post did too.
3. **Real Sociedad 2010, +1 goal.** Marca credits Ronaldo, La Liga and UEFA credit Pepe. It's why [Real Madrid says 451](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_career_achievements_by_Cristiano_Ronaldo#Records) and everyone else 450.
4. **Youth internationals, +18 goals for Ronaldo.** The fan list has him on 976 at 19 July, and his youth caps (7+5+1+3+2) add 18, which gives RSSSF's 994 exactly. Run that forward and the RSSSF method already puts him on 1,003 today. **No source publishes 1,003. That's my arithmetic, so treat it as UNCONFIRMED**, and I'd keep it off the site or label it clearly. Same method for Messi: 919 at 19 July, plus 27 from B teams and youth, is 946, two short of RSSSF's 948. I can't explain those two goals.

Spread for Ronaldo: 978 to 985 among senior counters, a gap of 7. RSSSF's official list runs 18 above the 985-style count on the same date. Spread for Messi: I found no live dispute over 937, but I could not read Transfermarkt's total, so UNCONFIRMED whether every counter agrees.

The lead holds under any choice. On the lowest senior count, Ronaldo is still about 41 ahead of Messi. Messi's 2026 pace (41 goals to Ronaldo's 28, FAN) is closing it slowly, and he has at least one more MLS match plus playoffs.
